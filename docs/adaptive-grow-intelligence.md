# GrowAI Phase 11: Adaptive Grow Intelligence & Action System

## Architectural Overview

The **Adaptive Grow Intelligence & Action System** builds on GrowAI Phases 7B through 10 to provide an evidence-based decision layer. It answers two primary questions for home and small-scale growers:

1. *"Given what I have recorded about this garden, what should I pay attention to now?"*
2. *"What is the most useful next action?"*

The system is strictly deterministic at its core, grounding every observation, recommendation, and priority in recorded facts without hallucination or autonomous medical/agronomic overreach.

---

## 1. Core Principles & Provenance Model

GrowAI enforces strict provenance tracking across all intelligence layers:

* **Recorded**: Direct grower measurements (e.g. planting date, sensor temperature, recorded NPK formulation, harvest weight).
* **Calculated**: Mathematical derivations from recorded facts (e.g. days since planting, historical average yield, shelf occupancy percentage).
* **Observed**: Visual sensory notes logged by the grower (e.g. fine webbing, chlorosis, root emergence).
* **Inferred**: Agronomic differential hypotheses generated by AI Doctor from visible symptoms.
* **Recommended**: Actionable guidance or inspection checks.
* **Unknown**: Parameters that have not been recorded or measured.

### Non-Negotiable Safety Invariants:
* **Unknown Remains Unknown**: Missing humidity or temperature is explicitly marked as unrecorded, never assumed suitable or unsuitable.
* **Zero Fabricated Dosages or PHIs**: Feeding recommendations never prescribe fertilizer amounts; guidance always references manufacturer product label directions. No synthetic chemical pesticides or pre-harvest intervals (PHIs) are invented.
* **Zero Guaranteed Yields or Survival**: Historical harvest quantities are reported as past facts, never promised as future guarantees.
* **Maturity Is Never Inferred Solely from Elapsed Calendar Days**: Visual checks prompt the grower to inspect for physical signs of readiness rather than declaring maturity automatically.
* **No Medical or Fatality Claims**: "Critical" priority strictly means *"Requires prompt grower attention based on the available recorded evidence"*, never asserting certainty of plant mortality.
* **Low-Confidence Hypotheses Are Never Escalated to Critical**: Unverified AI scan results are handled with cautious language prompting manual follow-up.
* **Mandatory Structural Safety**: Space and container load advisories always mandate checking balcony load limits and shelf ratings before adding weight.

## 2. System Architecture & Data Flow

```text
  Existing Garden Data
  (user_plants, growth_notes, photos, diagnoses, reminders, conditions, feedings, propagations, cycles, harvests, space)
                                 │
                                 ▼
                     ┌───────────────────────┐
                     │  Garden State Engine  │
                     └───────────────────────┘
                                 │
                                 ▼
                     ┌───────────────────────┐
                     │  Action Prioritizer   │
                     └───────────────────────┘
                                 │
                 ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                 ▼                               ▼
     ┌───────────────────────┐       ┌───────────────────────┐
     │   Next Best Action    │       │   Daily Grow Check    │
     └───────────────────────┘       └───────────────────────┘
                 │                               │
                 └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                 ▼
         ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
         │     UI Consumers & Grounded Query Engine      │
         │  (/app/grow-intelligence, PlantIntelligenceTab│
         └───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Garden State Engine** (`src/lib/adaptive-grow/garden-state-engine.ts`):
   Deterministic aggregation of health statuses, overdue care, unrecorded microclimate metrics, feeding records, production milestones, and data completeness.
2. **Action Prioritization Engine** (`src/lib/adaptive-grow/action-prioritizer.ts`):
   Transforms facts into prioritized `GrowAction` items across 9 categories (`health`, `care`, `environment`, `growth`, `feeding`, `production`, `propagation`, `space`, `data_quality`).
3. **"What Should I Do Now?" Engine** (`src/lib/adaptive-grow/next-best-action.ts`):
   Deterministically identifies ONE highest-value action based on priority weighting and category precedence (health > care > environment > production > growth > propagation > space > feeding > data quality). Returns `null` when no meaningful action is required.
4. **Daily Grow Check Engine** (`src/lib/adaptive-grow/daily-grow-check.ts`):
   Synthesizes a daily briefing headline, positive signals, observations to record, and missing data alerts without generic motivational fluff.
5. **Data Quality Engine** (`src/lib/adaptive-grow/data-quality.ts`):
   Audits record completeness across 6 categories (Plant Basics, Growth Tracking, Environmental Records, Nutrient Feeding, Health Follow-ups, Production Records). Strictly: **Data completeness is not plant health.**
6. **Grounded Query Engine** (`src/lib/adaptive-grow/query-engine.ts`):
   Answers grower questions (e.g., *"Which plants need attention?"*, *"What should I do today?"*, *"Which crops produced the most historically?"*, *"What information am I missing?"*) grounded strictly in user records.

## 3. UI Integrations & Progressive Disclosure

### Adaptive Dashboard (`/app/grow-intelligence`)
* **Today & Daily Grow Check**: Date-stamped status, headline, positive signals, and observation reminders.
* **Next Best Action Hero Callout**: Prominent action card displaying action guidance, direct navigation links, and explicit supporting evidence breakdown (`WHY -> ACTION`).
* **Prioritized Action Queue**: Categorized cards with quick-filter pills (`All`, `Health`, `Care`, `Environment`, `Growth`, `Feeding`, `Production`).
* **Natural-Language Query Widget**: Interactive "Ask Your Garden Records" search box with sample prompts and evidence-grounded response cards.
* **Record Completeness Bar**: Visual completion percentage across categories with actionable tips to improve tracking accuracy.
* **Preserved Phase 10 Sections**: Scientific Integrity Banner, Due Reminders, Conditions & Nutrition Highlights, Production Cycles & Yield Analytics, and Space & Structural Safety.

### Plant-Level Intelligence (`PlantIntelligenceTab.tsx`)
* Embedded in `MyGardenPage` under the Intelligence tab.
* Highlights **Recommended Next Action** for the selected plant.
* Provides **Supporting Evidence** cards linking recorded facts to recommended interventions.
* Displays **Unified State Matrix** across Health, Growth, Environment, Nutrition, and Harvests.

---

## 4. Offline & PWA Architecture

* All Phase 11 adaptive engines are pure TypeScript functions that execute deterministically on local data without requiring active network connectivity.
* In offline mode, the dashboard and plant tabs calculate state, actions, daily checks, and query answers directly from cached records in localStorage/IndexedDB.
* When reconnected, underlying mutations (reminders, observations, feedings) sync via existing Phase 9.2 reliable queue mechanisms.

---

## 5. Automated Verification

The Phase 11 test suite covers:
* `Garden State`: empty garden, single plant, multi-plant mixed health, missing data handling.
* `Action Prioritization`: health, care, environment, growth, feeding, production, propagation, space, and data quality.
* `Safety Invariants`: low-confidence AI hypothesis containment, zero dosage invention, zero promised yields, structural load verification.
* `Next-Best-Action`: deterministic priority hierarchy and zero false urgency.
* `Grounded Query Engine`: factual answers for health, next action, harvest yields, and data gaps.
